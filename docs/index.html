SAARC Bilateral Concessions Index

The SAARC Bilateral Benefits & Concessions Index measures preferential treatments, treaties, and special statuses granted mutually or unilaterally between the 8 member states of South Asia.

Benefit Level Weightings

Level 1 Multilateral Baseline (1 pt)

Standard SAARC agreements, baseline visa schemes (SVES), or multilateral exemptions applicable equally to all members.

Level 2 Substantial Concession (2 pts)

Subsidized quotas, university fee concessions (SAU domestic rates), bilateral currency swap windows, or SDF tourist exemptions.

Level 3 Deep Bilateral Treaty (3 pts)

Unrestricted freedom of movement, national work/residency treatment, mutual defense or comprehensive free transit pacts (e.g., India-Nepal 1950, India-Bhutan 1949/2007).

Official Legal Sources

Every concession links directly to verifiable statutory acts, Ministry of External Affairs portals, central bank regulations, or institutional charters.
